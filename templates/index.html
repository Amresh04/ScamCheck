      STAY SAFE
      
                
      Simple ways to avoid online fraud
      
            
      

            
      
                
      
                    🔐
                    
      Never share secret codes
      
                    
      Never share an OTP, password, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV, or card number with anyone.
      
                
      

                
      
                    🔗
                    
      Verify before clicking
      
                    
      Do not trust links that create urgency, fear, prizes, refunds, or fake account-blocking warnings.
      
                
      

                
      
                    ☎
                    
      Use official channels
      
                    
      Open official apps or type the official website manually instead of trusting a message link.
      
                
      

                
      
                    🔑
                    
      Use unique passwords and MFA
      
                    
      Create a different, long password for each important account. Turn on multi-factor authentication and never approve a sign-in you did not start.
      
                
      

                
      
                    💸
                    
      Pause before sending money
      
                    
      Verify payment requests with the person or business using a known number. Never pay a fee to receive a prize, refund, or job offer.
      
                
      

                
      
                    🛠
                    
      Keep devices protected
      
                    
      Install software updates, use a screen lock, and download apps only from trusted stores. Avoid opening unexpected attachments.
      
                
      

                
      
                    🧭
                    
      Check the sender and address
      
                    
      Look closely for misspelled domains and unexpected sender details. A familiar logo or HTTPS padlock alone does not prove a message is genuine.
      
                
      

                
      
                    🚨
                    
      Act quickly if you are targeted
      
                    
      Stop communicating, contact your bank through its official app or number if money or account access is at risk, then report and block the sender.
